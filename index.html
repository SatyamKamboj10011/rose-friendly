Rose Friendly is Radaur's most beloved dining destination — a kitchen where Haryanvi tradition meets bold Indo-Chinese flair. From overnight slow-cooked Dal Makhani and tandoor-charred Kababs to sizzling wok-fired Noodles and melt-in-your-mouth Chaap, every plate is a labour of love.

Serving the community since day one — breakfast, lunch, and dinner — with the warmth and honesty of a home-style kitchen.
